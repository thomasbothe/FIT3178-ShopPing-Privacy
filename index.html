ShopPing Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

What ShopPing collects

Your email address and the name you choose when you create an account. The household you belong to, its name, invite code and member names. Your shopping list, pantry, shopping trips and the stores you save (their names and map locations). A notification token for your device, so we can send you alerts. Your notification choices.

What stays on your device

Your own location. ShopPing uses it on your phone to notice when you arrive at a saved store, and to show stores near you when you add one. It is not uploaded or stored on our servers. Photos are never taken or saved: the camera is used only to read barcodes while the scanner is open.

How your information is used

To sign you in, to share your list and pantry with the people in your household, and to send the alerts you have switched on, such as when someone starts a shopping trip or adds an item. We do not use your information for advertising or tracking, and we do not sell it.

Who can see it

Other members of your household can see your name, and everything on the shared list, pantry and saved stores. Your email address is visible only to you. Nobody outside your household can read your household's data.

Services we use

Firebase, a Google service, stores accounts and data, and sends notifications. Open Food Facts receives the barcode or search words you enter when you look up a product. It does not receive your name, email or location.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings, then Delete Account. This removes you from your household, deletes your profile and your device's notification token, and deletes your account. If you are the last member, the household's list, pantry and stores are deleted too. Signing out also removes your device's notification token.

Your choices

You can turn off notifications in Settings, then Notifications, or in your iPhone's Settings. You can turn off location or camera access in your iPhone's Settings. The app keeps working, but arrival alerts and barcode scanning will not.

Children

ShopPing is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
